Privacy

by Elghaly LLC

Updated 22 November 2026

In short

Thoth collects nothing about you. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookie. We never receive what you type or say.

How الغالي answers

On this phone. The first time you choose it, Thoth downloads its model files (about 291 MB) from models.elghaly.dev, checks each one, and keeps them on this device. Like any web server, that server sees your IP address and which files were requested. After the download, what you type stays on this device.

With your own Gemini key. If you paste your own Gemini key, your messages and the key go directly from your browser to Google's Gemini service, under Google's terms. In the app, the key stays in this page's memory: it is cleared when you close the tab (and also when you reload the page), and it is never sent to us. The separate assistant page at /agent/ keeps the key in this tab's session storage until you close the tab.

Voice

The microphone is used after you tap it and allow it, never before. Turning speech into text uses your browser's speech service; in Chrome on Android, that audio is processed by Google. Reading answers aloud uses voices installed on your phone.

What stays on your device

Your language choice (a short language code, kept in your browser and in a small cookie so the page opens in your language), the look you chose and the name you typed for the greeting (kept in your browser, never sent anywhere), and the model files if you download them. Clear this site's data in your browser and they are gone. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily clear data kept by your browser.

This website

GitHub Pages serves this site. Like any host, it receives your IP address and the pages you request, under GitHub's privacy statement. We add no tracking of our own.

The Android app

The Android app opens this same website in a secure browser view. It asks for no permissions itself (the microphone is asked by the browser, on tap) and keeps no backup of its data.

Children

Thoth is made for exporters and businesses. It is not directed at children.
